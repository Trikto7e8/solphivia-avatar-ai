# Help build Solphivia IA

Solphivia IA 1.1 beta 3 is an animated Android companion with Italian voice, reading Stop/Resume, visible transcripts, attachments and encrypted portable personal memory.

## Where to help

- **Android reliability:** voice handoff, foreground/background lifecycle, battery, error recovery and accessibility. Coordinate in [#3](https://github.com/Trikto7e8/solphivia-avatar-ai/issues/3).
- **Testing:** use synthetic examples and share one reproducible observation in [#6](https://github.com/Trikto7e8/solphivia-avatar-ai/issues/6).
- **Animations and character:** improve contextual transitions, outfits and motion while respecting the artwork license.
- **Independent providers:** extend the existing Stage A boundary with separately reviewed adapters. LM Studio/Ollama connections remain future work; see [#5](https://github.com/Trikto7e8/solphivia-avatar-ai/issues/5).
- **iOS feasibility:** explore a native prototype and document platform limits; see [#4](https://github.com/Trikto7e8/solphivia-avatar-ai/issues/4).

Start with a comment naming the part you want to work on and a small first step. Italian and English are welcome. Community collaboration, not an advertised paid position. No passwords, signing keys, private recordings or real memories are needed.
