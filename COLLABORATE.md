# Help build Solphivia / Aiutaci a far crescere Solphivia

Solphivia is an animated AI assistant with a working Android alpha: chat, voice, attachments, encrypted portable memory and an avatar. We want to make it reliable, let users choose local or cloud models, and design a serious iPhone version.

We are looking for Android/Google Play contributors, iOS/SwiftUI developers, local-LLM integrators, accessibility reviewers, translators and testers. A focused review or one reproducible bug report is useful too.

## Where to start

See the [collaboration issues](https://github.com/Trikto7e8/solphivia-avatar-ai/issues?q=is%3Aissue+is%3Aopen+label%3A%22help+wanted%22). Pick an area, explain your proposed first step and discuss it before a large pull request. We do not require private contact details. Use issues and discussions for coordination.

The Android code exists today; iOS, additional model adapters and store publication are work ahead. Store acceptance is not guaranteed. Community participation does not imply compensation or a deadline commitment.

## Per chi parla italiano

Ciao! Solphivia funziona già come alpha Android e vorremmo farla crescere insieme. Cerchiamo una mano per una versione Android pronta per Google Play, un progetto iOS fatto bene e collegamenti a modelli locali o cloud.

Sai sviluppare, fare test, migliorare accessibilità o documentazione? Scegli una richiesta aperta e raccontaci da dove vorresti cominciare. Anche un contributo piccolo ma concreto è benvenuto.

## Share the project

Try the [latest alpha](https://github.com/Trikto7e8/solphivia-avatar-ai/releases/tag/v0.9.2-alpha2), star the repository if it is useful, and share this page with people interested in Android, SwiftUI, accessibility or local AI. Describe it as a community alpha, not a finished App Store/Google Play product.
