**«Hola Solphivia» → spoken reply → your command. No Send button.**

Solphivia Mobile alpha9 is available! The voice path has now been tried successfully on a Motorola: wake, reply, WhatsApp opening and automatic question submission.

[**Download the Android alpha9 APK**](https://github.com/Trikto7e8/solphivia-avatar-ai/releases/tag/v0.9.2-alpha9) · [Setup and voice controls](https://github.com/Trikto7e8/solphivia-avatar-ai/blob/main/docs/VOICE-SESSION.md) · [Join the project](https://github.com/Trikto7e8/solphivia-avatar-ai/blob/main/COLLABORATE.md)

Wake listening is local. After the greeting, Android transcribes one command and may use the network. WhatsApp opening stays on the device; questions go to the connected AI provider with recent text history, without automatically adding vault memory or attachments. This is a community alpha, not a fully offline assistant or a Google Play release. Long battery sessions, screen lock and other devices still need testing.

**Help us take the next step:**

- Android testers: try wake → command → Stop and share a small report in [#6](https://github.com/Trikto7e8/solphivia-avatar-ai/issues/6).
- Android developers: help with foreground-service reliability, accessibility, power measurements and [Google Play readiness #3](https://github.com/Trikto7e8/solphivia-avatar-ai/issues/3).
- iOS developers: help design a realistic [native iPhone prototype #4](https://github.com/Trikto7e8/solphivia-avatar-ai/issues/4).
- Local AI developers: help shape [provider adapters #5](https://github.com/Trikto7e8/solphivia-avatar-ai/issues/5). Provider-independent operation remains future work.

One reproducible test or a small focused pull request is welcome. Please use synthetic examples and keep personal voice recordings, conversations and credentials private.
