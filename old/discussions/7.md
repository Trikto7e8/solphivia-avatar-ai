# OLD — Help build Solphivia: Android, iOS and local AI / Cerchiamo collaboratori

Snapshot precedente all’aggiornamento 1.1 beta 3. [Discussione originale](https://github.com/Trikto7e8/solphivia-avatar-ai/discussions/7).

Ciao! Solphivia has a working Android alpha, and we would like to build the next steps together.

We are looking for people who can help with a serious app stores release, an iOS experience designed for iPhone, local/cloud model adapters, accessibility and testing. Small, focused contributions are welcome.

Start here:
- [Android / app stores](https://github.com/Trikto7e8/solphivia-avatar-ai/issues/3)
- [iOS / App Store feasibility](https://github.com/Trikto7e8/solphivia-avatar-ai/issues/4)
- [Local and cloud model adapters](https://github.com/Trikto7e8/solphivia-avatar-ai/issues/5)
- [A first device test report](https://github.com/Trikto7e8/solphivia-avatar-ai/issues/6)

The Android alpha is available today. iOS, additional providers and store approval are not available or promised yet. Software is MIT; character and artwork rights are separate. This is a community collaboration invitation, not an advertised paid position.

**In italiano:** l’app sta crescendo e ci farebbe piacere costruire i prossimi passi insieme. Sai sviluppare per Android o iOS, collegare modelli locali, fare test o migliorare l’accessibilità? Scegli una richiesta e raccontaci il primo contributo che vorresti fare. Anche una prova ben descritta è un aiuto concreto.
